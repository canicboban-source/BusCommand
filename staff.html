      SELF-HELP FIRST

      Help & recovery

      Try these steps in order before contacting support. Do not spam refresh.

      1. 1. Refresh data

        Reload the app once to re-sync plans and sessions. Prefer this button over random F5 spam.

      2. 2. Sign out and sign in

        If the session looks stuck, sign out and sign back in with your dispatcher email.

      3. 3. Check network

        Look at the small cloud status (bottom-left). If it shows offline or error, wait for connection, then do step 1 again.

        Current status: —

      4. 4. Drivers signed out in bulk

        Drivers sign back in with their personal code. If an account is inactive, Company Admin reactivates it. Do not wipe company data.

      Still broken? Contact support

      Only after steps 1–4. Opens email to the company contact with safe diagnostic context (no passwords or PINs).
