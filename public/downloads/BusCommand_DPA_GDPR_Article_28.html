Data Processing Agreement

GDPR Article 28 Compliant • Controller-Processor Relationship

Parties to This Agreement

Data Controller: [Transit Operator Company Name], a company incorporated under the laws of [Country], with registered office at [Address] ("Controller")

Data Processor: BusCommand Enterprise, a company providing SaaS fleet management services, with registered office at [Address] ("Processor")

1. Subject Matter and Duration

Article 1.1

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into pursuant to Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller.

Article 1.2

Subject Matter: The Processor shall provide fleet management, dispatch operations, driver shift scheduling, and related transit operational services to the Controller through the BusCommand SaaS platform.

Article 1.3

Duration: This DPA shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for the duration of the main service agreement between the parties, plus a period of twelve (12) months following termination of such agreement to allow for data export and deletion.

2. Nature and Purpose of Processing

Article 2.1

Categories of Data Subjects: The processing shall involve personal data of the following categories of data subjects:

  • Employees of the Controller (drivers, dispatchers, administrative staff)
  • Contractors and subcontractors of the Controller
  • Passengers and service users (where applicable for operational purposes)
Article 2.2

Categories of Personal Data: The Processor shall process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification data: names, employee identification numbers, contact information
  • Operational data: shift assignments, route assignments, operational status
  • Authentication data: PIN codes (hashed), access credentials
  • Communication data: incident reports, operational messages
Article 2.3

Purpose of Processing: The personal data shall be processed for the following purposes:

  • Fleet management and dispatch operations
  • Driver shift scheduling and assignment
  • Operational incident reporting and management
  • Compliance with EU Regulation 561/2006 on drivers' hours
  • Platform authentication and access control
  • Audit logging and compliance reporting

3. Obligations of the Processor

Article 3.1

Processing on Controller's Behalf: The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do so by European Union or Member State law to which the Processor is subject. In such case, the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

Article 3.2

Confidentiality: The Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

Article 3.3

Security Measures: The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including inter alia, as appropriate:

  • The pseudonymization and encryption of personal data
  • The ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems and services
  • The ability to restore the availability and access to personal data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident
  • A process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures for ensuring the security of the processing
Article 3.4

Subprocessor Engagement: The Processor shall not engage another processor without prior specific or general written authorization from the Controller. The Processor shall provide the Controller with a list of subprocessors already engaged, and shall inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of subprocessors, thereby giving the Controller the opportunity to object to such changes.

4. Data Subject Rights

Article 4.1

Assistance to Controller: The Processor shall assist the Controller, by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights laid down in Chapter III of the GDPR.

Article 4.2

Data Subject Rights: The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the following data subject rights:

  • Right of access (Article 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Article 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") (Article 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Article 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Article 21 GDPR)

5. Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)

Article 5.1

Encryption: The Processor shall implement the following encryption measures:

  • In Transit: All data transfers shall be encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher
  • At Rest: All data stored shall be encrypted using AES-256 encryption
Article 5.2

Data Residency: All personal data shall be stored and processed within the European Union. The Processor shall ensure that cloud infrastructure is hosted in the europe-west3 region (Frankfurt, Germany) unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Controller.

Article 5.3

Multi-Tenant Isolation: The Processor shall implement strict multi-tenant isolation to ensure that data of one Controller is not accessible to another Controller or any unauthorized party.

Article 5.4

Access Control: The Processor shall implement role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring that personnel have access only to data necessary for their roles.

Article 5.5

Audit Logging: The Processor shall maintain comprehensive, immutable append-only audit logs for all data processing operations for a minimum period of 24 months.

6. GDPR Article 88 & Right to Disconnect Compliance

Article 6.1

Right to Disconnect: The Processor shall ensure that the platform does not collect telemetry, location data, or any monitoring data outside of active shift periods. GPS tracking or continuous surveillance of drivers is not implemented or permitted.

Article 6.2

Working Time Compliance: The platform shall support compliance with EU working time regulations and EU Regulation 561/2006 on drivers' hours, including enforcement of rest periods and limitation of working hours.

Article 6.3

Off-Duty Privacy: Drivers and other data subjects shall not be monitored or tracked outside of their assigned working hours. The platform shall not collect or process personal data during periods when the data subject is not actively engaged in work duties.

7. Subprocessors

Article 7.1

Authorized Subprocessors: The Processor is authorized to engage the following subprocessors for the provision of the services:

Subprocessor Services Data Location
Google Cloud Platform Cloud infrastructure, database hosting, computational services Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3)
Article 7.2

Subprocessor Agreement: The Processor shall ensure that each subprocessor provides sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures in such a manner that processing will meet the requirements of the GDPR and ensure the protection of the rights of the data subject.

Article 7.3

Subprocessor Changes: The Processor shall notify the Controller of any intended addition or replacement of subprocessors at least 30 days prior to such change, providing the Controller with the opportunity to object to such changes.

8. Data Deletion and Return

Article 8.1

Deletion or Return: Upon termination of the services, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data processed on behalf of the Controller, and delete existing copies unless European Union or Member State law requires the storage of the personal data.

Article 8.2

Data Export: The Processor shall provide the Controller with the ability to export all personal data in a commonly used machine-readable format prior to termination of services.

Article 8.3

Verification: The Processor shall provide written confirmation to the Controller that all personal data has been deleted or returned in accordance with this Article 8.

9. Data Breach Notification

Article 9.1

Notification to Controller: The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach. The notification shall at least:

  • Describe the nature of the personal data breach including where possible, the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned
  • Communicate the name and contact details of the data protection officer or other contact point where more information can be obtained
  • Describe the likely consequences of the personal data breach
  • Describe the measures taken or proposed to be taken by the Processor to address the personal data breach, including measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects
Article 9.2

Documentation: The Processor shall maintain a record of all personal data breaches, including the facts relating to the personal data breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken.

10. Audit and Compliance

Article 10.1

Audit Rights: The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations set out in this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller.

Article 10.2

Compliance Certifications: The Processor shall maintain and provide evidence of relevant compliance certifications, including but not limited to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR compliance assessments.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

Article 11.1

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of [European Union Member State] and shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of [European Union Member State]. Any disputes arising under or in connection with this DPA shall be resolved in accordance with such laws.

Article 11.2

In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this DPA and the main service agreement, the provisions of this DPA shall prevail to the extent of such conflict.

12. General Provisions

Article 12.1

Entire Agreement: This DPA constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and representations, whether written or oral.

Article 12.2

Amendments: Any amendment or modification to this DPA shall be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

Article 12.3

Severability: If any provision of this DPA is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
