1. Before You Start

This guide replaces a live training session. Print it, pin it in the dispatch office, and hand a copy to every driver on their first shift. No prior software experience is required — every screen mirrors the steps below.

Support: If a step below does not match what you see on screen, contact your Company Admin first — they control which groups, buses, and permissions your account can see.

2. Dispatcher Cockpit — First 10 Minutes

Step 1 — Log in. Open the Staff link your Company Admin sent you, select your name and enter your PIN.
Step 2 — Open your group. The Operations Center shows all groups/lines you dispatch. Click a group card to open its Daily Plan.
Step 3 — Read "Needs Attention" first. This panel lists every open problem — uncovered shifts, missing buses, unconfirmed drivers, SOS alarms, and rest-period warnings — with a suggested fix next to each one.
Step 4 — Replace a driver or bus. Click the empty slot in the Daily Plan, pick a suggested replacement from the list (already filtered to available, in-group candidates), and confirm. The server saves the change immediately — no separate "save" button.
Step 5 — Check the dashboard automatically refreshes. The Operations Center updates itself every 30 seconds; the "last updated" timestamp in the top-right tells you how fresh the data is.

3. Driver App — First 5 Minutes

The Driver App is a Progressive Web App (PWA): open the link once, no app-store install, no update prompts.

Step 1 — Log in. Open the Driver link, select your name and enter your PIN. First-time drivers set a personal PIN during activation.
Step 2 — Confirm today's shift. Your route, bus number, and start/end time are on the home screen. Tap "Confirm Shift" — the dispatcher sees this instantly.
Step 3 — Know your two quick-report buttons. "Delay / Traffic (+15 min)" and "Vehicle Fault" send a one-tap report to the dispatcher, with GPS-free location context. Nothing else on the home screen needs an explanation.
Step 4 — Know the SOS button. Press and hold for 2 seconds to alert the dispatcher immediately. If pressed by mistake, you have 30 seconds to cancel it from the same screen.
Step 5 — Check messages and calendar. Dispatcher messages appear at the bottom of the home screen; the Calendar tab shows the full month with color-coded shift types.

4. Company Admin — What You Configure Once

  • Groups/lines, buses, and drivers (Driver Roster Template available in the Download Hub)
  • Duty catalog / Service Plan (the shift codes dispatchers assign from)
  • Dispatcher accounts and which groups each one can see
  • Company branding (name, logo, colors shown to your team)

Company Admin does not import monthly driver assignments — that stays with the dispatcher, by design, to keep day-to-day operational changes close to the people running the shift.

5. Frequently Asked in the First Week

QuestionShort answer
Can I edit a future day's plan?Daily Plan is read-only more than 0 days ahead — edit future days from the Monthly Plan instead.
What happens if two dispatchers edit the same shift?The server rejects the second save with a conflict message — refresh and reapply.
Can I see another company's data?No — every account is scoped to one tenant. This is enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI.
Where do I download official templates?The Download Hub on the BusCommand website — Driver Roster, Monthly Schedule, and Fleet templates in both XLSX and CSV.
